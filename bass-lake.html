Work / Bass Lake

Bass Lake

2026

A small 2D fly fishing game. You paddle a kayak, you cast a line, you wait.

Play it Source

I love fishing and I wanted to make something that captured the slow part of it. Not the catch, not the reel, the part before that. Hold the mouse to false-cast, the line whips back and forth and gets longer with each loop. Release to drop the fly. Wait. Switch to a different fly if you're getting ignored.

Dry flies bring up bluegill and pumpkinseed. Nymphs catch crappie. Wooly buggers get the bass to look up. The wrong fly gets nothing.

How it's built

It's pure p5.js and Canvas2D, no build step. The lake shape is generated with marching squares over a 2D noise field, with three basin centers connected by river paths. Every run is a different shore.

The whole static world (the trees, the lake polygon, the surface texture, the shore line) gets baked once into a downscaled image at startup. Per-frame rendering is just a sub-region blit plus animated layers, which keeps the framerate stable when you zoom in close.

Panfish use boids flocking with a spatial hash. Bass run a small state machine: they lurk near cover, get curious, sometimes commit to a strike. The fly line itself is a quadratic bezier with an animated control point, so you can see it load and unload during the false casts.

Multiplayer is wired through Supabase. Friends paddle the same lake, see each other casting.

Controls

W / S paddle forward and back
A / D turn the kayak
hold mouse false-cast (the line grows with each cycle)
release mouse deliver the fly to the cursor
click on a fish reel in
1 / 2 / 3 switch fly: dry fly, nymph, wooly bugger
scroll zoom
