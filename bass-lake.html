Work / Bass Lake

Bass Lake

A small 2D fly fishing game. You paddle a kayak, you cast a line, you wait.

Play it Source

I wanted to make something that captured the slow part of fishing. The part before the catch.

Procedural lake. Boids flocking for panfish. A small state machine for the bass. Multiplayer over Supabase. Pure p5.js, no build step.
