AI & Machine Learning

Students train tiny classifiers, study algorithmic bias, build intuition for how these systems work.

Safety & Ethics

Digital citizenship woven through every unit. Personal rubrics, contested cases, careful thinking.

Modular VEX Robotics

A standardized base robot with swappable sensor and mechanism modules. Lets a class of 24 get running in a single period instead of losing days to rebuilds. Curriculum scaffolds students from sensor basics through autonomous navigation with challenge-based assessments.
