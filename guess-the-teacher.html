Guess That Teacher

2024

A small game for spirit week. Students see a clue and try to figure out which teacher it belongs to.

Teachers submit a clue about themselves. A childhood pet, a favorite food, a hidden talent. Students see the clue and pick the teacher they think it belongs to. Right or wrong, they get a short blurb back, in the teacher's own words.

It runs on the lobby screens during spirit week, in homerooms as a warm-up, and on student devices as a pick-up game. The first version was a Computer Club test in the makerspace. They were arguing about which teacher had the yellow parakeet within thirty seconds. That's when I knew it would work.

Architecturally it's deliberately simple: a static front-end, a small JSON file, GitHub Pages. Any teacher can fork the repo and run their own version at their school without setting up infrastructure.
