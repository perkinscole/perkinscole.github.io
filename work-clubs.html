Work / Clubs

Clubs

After-school spaces for things that wouldn't fit in a class period.

Computer Club

2022–

An open room where students dig into whatever part of computing they care about. Coding, robotics, game design, hardware, 3D printing. Club members have built VEX competition robots, made games, and contributed to school-wide projects like Care Garden. The rule is simple: bring an idea, leave with something built.

Camera Club

2023–

A club built around visual storytelling. Photography basics, composition, editing, and the habit of really looking. Students document school events, build personal portfolios, and curate student photo shows that rotate through the lobby. A creative practice with a low barrier to entry and a clear place the work can land.
