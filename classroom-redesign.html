Classroom Redesign

A room redesigned with help from the students who use it.

Fluorescents, rows of identical tables, no soft seating, robotics getting torn down every period. Before I changed anything I asked the students.

Three questions. The answers were consistent. Lights too bright. Nowhere to sit with a group. Robotics work that never got to finish.

I changed the lights, broke the rows into three zones (soft seating, group tables, individual desks), and carved out a permanent corner for robotics where the field can stay assembled for weeks.

The most useful part was asking first. Most of what I would have done wasn't on their list. Most of what they asked for wasn't on mine.
