In the Workshop

Things I'm still figuring out.

3D-Printable Classroom Drones

A small drone you can print, build, and program in middle school.

Built around the micro:bit v2. Block-codable, low cost, and printable on a regular school printer. The goal is aerial robotics in a middle school classroom without the price tag or complexity of commercial kits. Students print the frame, assemble the electronics, and program the autonomous patterns.

Multiplayer Scratch

Adding real-time multiplayer to Scratch.

A networking layer that lets Scratch projects accept players from other devices. Students build a game in Scratch and their classmates can join. I'm interested in what happens to student motivation when code becomes a social medium, when the thing you make can have other people inside it.

Remy

A classroom robot with a face.

Remy runs on a Jetson Orin Nano, which can handle real computer vision and ML on-device. The idea is for Remy to be a shared classroom character students can interact with, program, and extend. A physical anchor for the AI and robotics units. Abstract concepts get a face. And wheels.
