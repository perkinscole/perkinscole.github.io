About

I studied history and computer science at Hobart College. After that I spent a year writing software for banks in New York, then drove across the country for a year, then worked at a compost company in North Carolina. I came back to teaching by accident, started as a special education aide, and ended up running the computer science program at Robert Adams Middle School in Holliston, Massachusetts. I've been there since 2022.

I make things mostly out of habit. Some of them are for work: a curriculum, a fish-counting dashboard, a school communication platform. Some of them are for the school: a digital garden in the lobby, a game for spirit week, a redesigned classroom with better lights. Some of them are just for me: a fishing game where you paddle a kayak around a small procedural lake.

The next thing I'd like to learn is architecture. Or design+build. Or whatever the right word is for the work where a room and the way it feels are the same problem. I notice that everything I love about teaching turns out to be about space and ritual and how a room works for the people in it. That feels like a thread worth pulling on.

Outside of all that, I play guitar, hike with my dog Jaco, cook with my partner Sammie, fish whenever I can, and visit a national park when I get the chance.
