About

I'm a middle school computer science teacher at Robert Adams Middle School in Holliston, Massachusetts. I run the CS program for grades 6 through 8.

I studied history and computer science at Hobart College. Before teaching I worked as a solutions architect at Appway in New York and in operations at CompostNow in Raleigh, North Carolina.

My next move is into architecture or design+build.

Outside of work: guitar, fishing, hiking with my dog Jaco, cooking with my partner Sammie, and national parks.
