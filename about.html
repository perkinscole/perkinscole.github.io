About

Background & Philosophy

My path has wound through history, computer science, software development, special education, cross-country road trips, and now a middle school classroom where I teach 500+ students a year to think computationally, create responsibly, and ask good questions about the technology that shapes their lives.

I studied History and Computer Science at Hobart College, where I discovered that the most interesting problems sit at the boundary of technical systems and human experience. After graduating I worked as a Solutions Architect at Appway in New York, designing business-process applications and learning how to translate between engineering teams and non-technical stakeholders. A gap year traveling 33 states deepened my sense of curiosity and confirmed that I wanted work centered on people, not just code. I spent time working at CompostNow in Raleigh, NC, where I helped develop safety policies and more efficient operations.

I returned to education, first as a special education instructional assistant, then as the computer science and digital literacy teacher at Robert Adams Middle School in Holliston, MA. I built the full 6–8 CS curriculum from scratch, covering everything from AI ethics to app development to interactive art. My classroom is a place where students build real things, collaborate across differences, and see technology as a tool for expression and community.

What Drives Me

I believe the best learning happens when people feel seen, challenged, and connected to something larger than themselves. Whether I'm designing a curriculum unit, building an interactive PBIS installation, or mentoring a robotics team, I'm always asking: How does this experience feel? Who is it serving? What does it make possible?

Where I'm Headed

I'm in the middle of a career transition toward architecture and design+build, work where physical space, craft, and human experience all sit on the same table. The throughline from teaching to building has been the same for me, designing things that help people learn, gather, and feel at home in the spaces they spend time in.

Interests

Architecture and the built environment, learning-space design, interactive and generative art, creative coding (p5.js, Processing, Arduino), game design, and community-centered technology.

Outside the Classroom

I play guitar, hike with my dog Jaco, cook with my partner Sammie, fish, and visit national parks whenever I can.
