For the Students

Small apps and games built for the kids I teach.

Rams Care

live

A student-facing companion to our PBIS program.

Students submit acts of kindness, self-reflections, and community contributions through a simple interface. The data feeds into a dashboard for staff. Designed with middle school usability in mind: big touch targets, minimal text, in and out in under a minute so it fits between classes.

Guess That Teacher

2024

A small game for spirit week. More.

Students see a clue (a childhood pet, a favorite food, a hidden talent) and try to figure out which teacher it belongs to. Runs on lobby screens during spirit weeks. Open source so other schools can fork it.
