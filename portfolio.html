Care Garden

An interactive installation in the school lobby. Built in p5.js.

Version 2, in progress for this year

Students smile at a webcam, and each smile grows a flower with their face at its center. The garden is rebuilt as a 3D block world, with a day and night cycle, weather, and characters from the school.

Version 1

Built for our PBIS Care Week. Students submitted acts of kindness through a kiosk in the lobby. Each submission generated a unique flower in a shared digital garden displayed on a large screen. Over the course of the week, thousands of submissions came in.
