Care Garden

An interactive installation in the school lobby. Built in p5.js.

An interactive installation built for our PBIS Care Week. Students smile at a webcam in the lobby, and each smile grows a flower in a shared garden on a large screen. Thousands of flowers grew over the week.

Built in p5.js. The garden is drawn as pixel art, with a day and night cycle, weather, and characters from the school. The current installation runs from a local server with richer data and display options than the original sketch.
