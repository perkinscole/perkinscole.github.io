Care Garden

A generative installation in the school lobby. Built in p5.js.

An interactive installation built for our PBIS Care Week. Students submitted acts of kindness through a kiosk in the lobby. Each submission generated a unique flower in a shared digital garden displayed on a large screen. Over the course of the week, thousands of submissions came in.

Built in p5.js. The current installation runs from a local server with richer data and display options than the original sketch.
